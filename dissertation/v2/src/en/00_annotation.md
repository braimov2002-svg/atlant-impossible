# ANNOTATION

The effectiveness of anticancer radiotherapy is still limited by the tolerance of healthy tissues, and one strategy to optimise the dose delivered in external radiotherapy is the use of radiosensitizers. This work studies the radio-enhancement effect of silver (Ag) in biological matter irradiated with kilovoltage x-rays and quantifies it by the dose enhancement factor (DEF). The spectrum of a 45 kV copper-anode x-ray tube needed for the DEF calculations is modelled as a function of the tube design, technical parameters and filters.

The literature on radiosensitization by high-Z elements, the interaction of kilovoltage photons with biological matter and x-ray spectrum models is reviewed. A semi-analytical thick-target model of the copper-anode tube spectrum is formulated, together with methods of calculating the air kerma, beam quality and DEF. The photon interaction cross-sections of silver, the mass attenuation coefficients of soft tissue, brain, bone, water and silver, an x-ray irradiation facility based on the REIS-I generator and the radiation-safety requirements are described. The spectrum of a 45 kVp tube with a 100 µm copper transmission anode was calculated with the Kramers formulas without a filter and with 30 µm and 2.11 mm aluminium filters. The self-filtration of the anode suppresses the photons between the Cu K-edge (8.98 keV) and about 14 keV, and the 2.11 mm filter removes the Cu K lines and concentrates the spectrum above the Ag K-edge (25.514 keV). The DEF of soft tissue grows linearly with the mass fraction of the element; for 1 % Ag it increases from 1.40 with the thin filter to 1.98 with the thick one. With thin filtration samarium (Sm), whose L-edges lie just below the Cu K lines, is more effective than silver, whereas with thick filtration silver is more effective, because its K-edge, unlike that of samarium (46.83 keV), lies inside the spectrum. The results support the choice of filtration and exposure conditions for experiments with Ag-containing radiosensitizers.

**Keywords:** x-ray tube spectrum; copper anode; bremsstrahlung; characteristic radiation; filtration; silver; samarium; radiosensitization; dose enhancement factor; kilovoltage x-rays.
