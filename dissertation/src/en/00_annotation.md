# ANNOTATION

The effectiveness of anticancer radiotherapy is still limited by the tolerance of healthy tissues, and one strategy to optimise the dose delivered in external radiotherapy is the use of radiosensitizers. This dissertation studies the radio-enhancement effect of silver (Ag) in biological matter irradiated with kilovoltage x-rays and quantifies it by the dose enhancement factor (DEF). The spectrum of a 45 kV copper-anode x-ray tube needed for the DEF calculations is modelled as a function of the tube design, its technical parameters and its filters. For this purpose, a semi-analytical thick-target model with a single fitted parameter (the developed model, DM) was created and validated against the SpekPy v2 toolkit. Mass energy-absorption coefficients were taken from the NIST tables, and the analytical DEF values were verified by Monte Carlo photon-transport calculations.

The unfiltered spectrum is dominated by the Cu K lines at 8.0–8.9 keV (68.5 % of the photons), so the DEF for 1 % Ag by mass is only 1.20 and remains within 1.19–1.21 when the anode angle, tube voltage or beryllium window is changed. Added filtration is the decisive parameter: 1 mm Al raises the DEF to 1.48, 2 mm Al to 1.62 and 0.2 mm Cu to 1.80, while the air kerma decreases 120–1300-fold. At equal transmission, light and medium-Z filters such as Al and Ti are optimal, whereas K-edge filters with edges at 20–30 keV (Mo, Ag, Sn) are the least effective at strong filtration. The DM reproduces SpekPy within 3.5–4.6 % in half-value layer for filtered beams and within 1–2 % in DEF. The DEF increases with depth (beam hardening) and decreases inside a heavily loaded region (self-shielding); Monte Carlo calculations confirm the analytical model within 1–10 %. Per unit mass, Ag is a weaker physical sensitizer than Au, Pt, Bi or Gd and is comparable to iodine. The results provide a basis for choosing the filtration and exposure conditions in experiments with Ag-containing radiosensitizers.

**Keywords:** x-ray tube spectrum; copper anode; bremsstrahlung; characteristic radiation; filtration; silver; radiosensitization; dose enhancement factor; kilovoltage x-rays; Monte Carlo method.
